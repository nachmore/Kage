Tool: Situation: Hover or drag a mascot

What's wrong with the current animations (from the frame art + mascot.js)

  1. Wave ends in a snap. It plays f1→f5 and cuts straight back to f1. f5 is a full head tilt, so the head jerks upright.
  2. The cat shrinks when it starts thinking. Jump frames draw the cat at less than half the idle size, and the switch to the 60px jump box is a hard cut.
  3. Uniform frame timing. Every frame is held equally (250ms wave, 125ms hop), with no anticipation, no hang at the apex and no landing beat. The hop's spacing is nearly linear on the way up.
  4. Thinking never rests. The 8 fps hop loops back-to-back for as long as the agent works, which gets frantic on long waits.
  5. Idle is a still image. Between waves (every 10±2s) nothing moves: no breathing, no blinks.
  6. No reduced-motion support anywhere in the app.
  7. Unused art. 50 poses in ui/assets (happy, winking, interested, sleeping, magnifying-glass…) that the mascot never uses.
